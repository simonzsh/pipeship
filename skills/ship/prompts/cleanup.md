# Cleanup brief

You are a cleanup subagent. You get your chunk worktree. In quick mode you get the worktree of every chunk, and you commit in each one.

Look for: simplification, dead code, repo conventions, readability. Use the `simplify` and `code-review` skills if available. You work in your worktree, run only the fast checks, and commit.

In quick mode you cover the whole diff, every chunk, with the same checks.
